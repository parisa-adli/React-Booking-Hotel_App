## Changes made

Generated by `npm run data:import` (`scripts/import-insideairbnb.mjs`):

- **Subset only:** 25 listings per city (100 in total). Listings qualify if they have
  a photo, coordinates, a price ≤ 2,000, a rating, at least 5 reviews and some
  availability. They are ranked by rating weighted by review count.
- **Fields reduced** to what the app displays (see `docs/PRD.md` §8.1). Host bios,
  host profile URLs and pictures, license numbers and other columns are dropped.
  Host names are cut to the first name.
- **Transformed:** HTML stripped from descriptions (and capped at 1,200 characters),
  prices parsed to numbers in the city's local currency, amenities parsed and turned
  into boolean filter flags.

Prices and availability are a **snapshot** from the dates above, not live data.
Listing photos are served by Airbnb's CDN and remain the property of their owners.
This project is not affiliated with Airbnb or Inside Airbnb.

`bookmarks` are the app's own demo data.
